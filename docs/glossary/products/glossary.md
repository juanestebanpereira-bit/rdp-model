# Products — Glossary

Subject-area terms for the Products component (Product Hierarchy). Unlike
`rdp-docs/docs/glossary/glossary.md`, which covers ecosystem-wide terms,
this glossary is scoped to one subject area and is assembled into
customer-facing sites at build time alongside glossaries from other subject
areas.

The hierarchy runs broadest to most granular: Department → Class → Style →
Item.

**Department**
The broadest level of the product hierarchy. Used to report and analyze
performance at the highest level of the classification structure.

**Class**
A grouping of styles within a department. Used to analyze performance
across all styles and items it contains.

**Style**
A grouping of items within a class. Used to analyze performance across all
items it contains.

**Item**
The primary unit of merchandise, and the level at which all transactional
data — sales, inventory, purchasing, orders — is recorded. Rolls up through
style, class, and department.
